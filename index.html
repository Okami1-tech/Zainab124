Chapter I · The first word
♡
LOVING

"My baby's love is not on the surface of this world. It's beyond."

My love, I wish you could see what happens inside my heart whenever I think of you.

Some feelings are too deep for ordinary words. You are one of mine.

Chapter II · Your safe place
∞
SUPPORTIVE

"He gives me strength when I cannot stand alone. I lean on him when I am weak. I rested on his lap when I am tired."

My Queen, you don't always have to be strong.

When life feels heavy, I want you to know that you can rest. I want my presence to be a place where your heart finds peace.

Chapter III · The promise
♾
LOYAL

"His love don't change with his mood. He always love me."

That sentence stayed with me.

I want you to experience a love that doesn't disappear when things get difficult.

Truth, faithfulness and consistency. Not just promises, but the way I live my love for you.

CARING

"Should I cry in this part? I can't explain."

Maybe it's the little things that say the most.

Your rest matters. Your worries matter. Your happiness matters.

And I never want you to feel that you have to explain why something matters before I care.

Chapter V · The foundation
☾
GOD-FEARING

"I really love that you put Allah first in everything you do."

Of all the things you said, this one reminds me of what matters most.

Every good thing in me is a blessing from Allah. I pray He protects my heart from pride and keeps me sincere.

May He guide us in every step.
